## **Brands**

### **PeppolTR**

PeppolTR is a reliable Peppol e-Invoice solution that enables you to send and receive e-invoices via the Peppol network in compliance with international standards.

[Learn More](https://peppoltr.com/)

### **JetSRM**

JetSRM Supplier Portal is a Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) software integrated with SAP that enables businesses to perform their business processes with their suppliers faster, easier and more integrated.

[Learn More](https://jetsrm.com/en/home-page/)

### **MIP**

MIP is an integration solution that enables companies to integrate on-premises, cloud and hybrid systems. MIP offers comprehensive functionality that makes it easy to create, deploy and manage integration processes and data flows across the entire organization.

[Learn More](https://usemip.com/)

### **oneri.io**

oneri.io is an AI-enhanced Continuous Improvement solution that includes an employee suggestion system, kaizen, 5S and near-miss modules. oneri.io aims to support companies in their Continuous Improvement efforts.

[Learn More](https://oneri.io/en)
